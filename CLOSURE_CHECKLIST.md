## Technical Summary

### Why the Project Closed
**Garena OB51 Patch Changes**:
- Level 2 accounts: ❌ Cannot send likes (previously could)
- Level 9 accounts: ⚠️ ~20 like limit
- Level 15 accounts: ⚠️ ~200 like limit
- Level 40+ accounts: ✅ No limit (but expensive to obtain)

**Our Service Model**:
- Relied on **automated Level 2 guest account generation**
- Provided **free unlimited likes** to users
- **No longer viable** after OB51 changes
